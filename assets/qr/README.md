# Benny’s website QR code

Destination: https://bennystreeservices.com/

- `bennys-website-qr.png`: dark green QR with the supplied logo, 1640 × 1640 pixels, 300 DPI.
- `bennys-website-qr.svg`: scalable print version with the logo embedded, no external asset required.
- `bennys-website-qr-plain.png` and `.svg`: black-and-white versions without a center logo.
- `bennys-website-qr-print-card.png`: branded 1800 × 2290 pixel card with a heading, website and primary phone number.

These are static QR codes pointing straight to the website. They do not depend on a QR-code subscription or redirect service. They are not Google review links.

Keep the white border and square proportions when placing the QR on business cards, flyers or signs. Test the final printed copy with a phone before ordering a full batch.

Generated by `scripts/build_qr.py` with high error correction. The script independently decodes the final logo and plain PNGs, the print card, reduced copies and JPEG-compressed copies using ZXing. All 14 checks passed. Python build dependencies are Pillow, qrcode and zxing-cpp; the deployed website does not need these libraries.
